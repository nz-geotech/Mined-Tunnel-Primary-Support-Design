References

Principal references for the preliminary weak-rock tunnel support methodology and associated ground–support interaction concepts:

  1. Hoek, E. and Brown, E.T. (1980). Underground Excavations in Rock. Institution of Mining and Metallurgy, London.
  2. Hoek, E. (1999). Support for very weak rock associated with faults and shear zones.
  3. Hoek, E. (2008). The 2008 Kersten Lecture: Integration of Geotechnical and Structural Design in Tunneling.
  4. Vlachopoulos, N. and Diederichs, M.S. (2009a). Improved longitudinal displacement profiles for convergence-confinement analysis of deep tunnels. Rock Mechanics and Rock Engineering. Accepted, in press (as cited); 16 pp.
  5. Vlachopoulos, N. and Diederichs, M.S. (2009b). Comparison of 2D and 3D analysis methods for single and paired tunnels in weak highly stressed ground. Manuscript intended for submission to Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology (as cited); 40 pp.

These references provide methodological context; individual equations and assumptions must be checked against their original sources before design application.

Engineering design must be checked for ground model, construction sequence, groundwater, load combinations, support installation and applicable standards.
